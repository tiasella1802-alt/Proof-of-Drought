      Built for COP31

      COP31 meets in Antalya in November 2026. Its Pre-COP ministers met in October in Nadi, Fiji, one of the eight districts this cover protects. It builds on parametric insurance already working in the Pacific (PCRIC, UNDP's PICAP).
