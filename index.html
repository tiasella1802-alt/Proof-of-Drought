Drought insurance for Fiji's sugarcane farmers

When the rain fails, the money arrives. Automatically.

Satellites watch the rain over every cane district. When two independent records agree a drought has hit, a smart contract pays every insured farmer straight to their phone. No forms, no assessors, no waiting.

      Real

      45 years of daily rainfall and soil moisture for Fiji's 8 cane districts from Copernicus ERA5 and NASA POWER · Fiji's cane harvests (FAOSTAT) · the 1998 timeline (UN OCHA) · the smart contract, running on a real Ethereum virtual machine in your browser

      Simulated

      The 96 farmers, their wallets and the money are test versions · district points are approximate · the payout rules are this project's proposal; a real scheme would set them with an insurer and the regulator

      Built for COP31

      COP31 meets in Antalya in November 2026. Its Pre-COP ministers met in October in Nadi, Fiji, one of the eight districts this cover protects. It builds on parametric insurance already working in the Pacific (PCRIC, UNDP's PICAP).
